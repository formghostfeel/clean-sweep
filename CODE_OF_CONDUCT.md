# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive: empathy, respect for differing views, constructive feedback,
graceful acceptance of mistakes, focus on community well-being.

Unacceptable: sexualized language or advances, trolling, personal or
political attacks, harassment, publishing private information, other
conduct unreasonable in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards and take fair
corrective action against inappropriate behavior.

## Scope

Applies to all community spaces (GitHub, Discord, Telegram) and official
representations of the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abuse to **conduct@clean-sweep.dev**. All complaints are reviewed
promptly and fairly; reporter privacy is respected.
